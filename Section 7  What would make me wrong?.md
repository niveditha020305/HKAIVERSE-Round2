Labs that effectively cover CI/CD, Docker, cloud computing, and automation.

7b. One Belief That Changed During the Analysis

Initial Belief:

I initially believed that DevOps theory was not very important and that practical laboratory work alone would be sufficient to learn DevOps skills.

What Changed My Mind:

During the curriculum analysis, I understood that theory alone does not explain real deployment workflows, and practical activities are necessary to apply concepts such as CI/CD, Docker, cloud computing, and automation. I also realized that theoretical understanding helps students understand how and why these tools are used.

Updated Belief:

I now believe that DevOps theory and practical laboratory work should be taught together. This combination can help students develop conceptual understanding, apply industry tools, and become better prepared for modern software development environments.
